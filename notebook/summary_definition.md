Notes:
-The reader should understand the company in roughly 30–60 seconds.
-The summary should prioritise decision-relevant information.
-It should highlight material risks without exaggerating them.
-It should explicitly state important missing information.
-It should remain factual and avoid making the final approval decision.
-It should direct attention to matters that warrant closer review.

## Intended reader

An internal team vetting companies as potential suppliers or project partners.

## Decision it supports

Whether the company appears suitable for closer review and which matters require further investigation before any business relationship is considered.

## Role of the summary

Provide a concise, factual overview of the company's identity, business activity, status and material ownership structure.

Highlight decision-relevant financial trends, cash flow, liquidity, leverage, audit findings and litigation without reproducing the entire record.

Clearly communicate missing information and matters that warrant closer review.

## What the summary is not

It does not approve or reject the company, perform investment analysis or predict future performance.

It does not assess management quality without evidence, treat allegations as confirmed outcomes, or replace the full record and human due-diligence process.
